# CheckFirst

**Think Before You Click. Verify Before You Pay.**

CheckFirst is an Android application designed to help people stay safer while using digital payments and financial services.

With the growth of UPI payments, online transactions, and digital lending, people are increasingly exposed to fake payment requests, phishing links, QR-code scams, and misleading loan offers. We built CheckFirst to help users recognize these risks and make better financial decisions.

## Features

- **Scam Shield:** Helps identify suspicious messages and possible scam attempts.
- **Link Analyzer:** Checks links for suspicious domains and other warning signs.
- **QR & UPI Analyzer:** Scans QR codes and helps users understand payment details before proceeding.
- **Loan Calculator:** Helps users calculate loan repayments and understand borrowing costs.
- **Loan Risk Checker:** Helps users identify possible risks in loan offers and lending applications.
- **AI Assistant:** Uses AI to explain financial-safety information and help users understand potential risks.
- **Pause & Verify:** Encourages users to check important details before making payments or sharing information.
- **Emergency Help:** Provides access to Indian cybercrime reporting resources, including the 1930 helpline.

## Privacy and Safety

We understand that financial information is sensitive. CheckFirst is designed with user privacy in mind.

- The app does not ask users to share their UPI PIN, OTP, password, or CVV.
- User content should not be uploaded automatically for analysis.
- Important warnings are shown to help users make informed decisions.
- AI-generated explanations are intended to assist users, not replace official verification.

## Emergency Support

For suspected financial cyber fraud in India:

- **Helpline:** 1930
- **Reporting website:** https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/

Users should also contact their bank immediately if they have lost money.

## Important Note

CheckFirst is a financial-safety assistance tool. Its results are not a guarantee that a message, website, QR code, or loan offer is safe or fraudulent. Users should always verify important financial information through official sources.

## Our Goal

We built CheckFirst with one simple idea in mind: people should have a chance to recognize a financial scam before it costs them money.

We hope this application encourages people to pause, check, and think before making financial decisions.
